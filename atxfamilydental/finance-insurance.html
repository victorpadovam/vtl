How we help

A little more clarity before you sit in the chair.

Our goal is to make the financial side of dental care as straightforward as possible.

01 · INSURANCE VERIFICATION

We check your benefits.

When you schedule, we’ll collect your insurance information, verify eligibility and benefits with your provider, and file your insurance claim.

02 · ESTIMATE OF COSTS

You’ll know what to expect.

Before treatment, we’ll let you know expected out-of-pocket costs for non-covered portions. Even if we’re out-of-network, we can still check your benefits.

A few things to keep in mind

Estimates are not guarantees.

Insurance companies provide estimates of coverage, not guarantees. Your policy is a contract between you and your carrier, and claims may sometimes be paid differently than expected.
