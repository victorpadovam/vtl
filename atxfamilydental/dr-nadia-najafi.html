Her story

Expertise with a reassuring presence.

Dr. Nadia completed a rigorous General Practice Residency at Swedish Medical Center in Seattle, where she received focused training in advanced surgical procedures, sedation dentistry, and the care of medically complex patients.

During her residency, she performed hundreds of surgical procedures including wisdom tooth extractions, implants, and bone grafting.

Her approach is thoughtful and patient-centered, with an emphasis on clear communication, comfort, and trust. She works closely with patients to help them feel informed and confident in their treatment decisions.

Dr. Nadia earned her dental degree from Texas A&M (Baylor) College of Dentistry and her undergraduate degree in biology from the University of Texas at Dallas.
