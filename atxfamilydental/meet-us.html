Thoughtful care.

Welcome to ATX Family Dental, a staple in the Austin dental community since 2016.

We’ve put a lot of thought into your visit so you feel informed, comfortable, and cared for.

We believe educated patients make the best decisions, so we’re focused on giving you the full picture — how your oral health connects to your overall well-being and why addressing small issues now can help prevent bigger problems later.

With advanced technology and personalized care, we focus on results that look great and last. More than that, we see you as part of our community, not just a patient. We’re invested in your health, your confidence, and your experience.

Since 2016, we’ve been living, working, and growing right here in 78704. This is our home, and we’re proud to be part of the neighborhood.
