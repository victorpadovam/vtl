The ATX approach

A Subtle, Stunning Smile Upgrade

Veneers are a game-changer for your smile, but we believe in a conservative approach—enhancing your natural teeth, not replacing them. Using ultra-thin, custom-crafted porcelain, we refine shape, color, and symmetry for a look that’s seamless and natural.

What veneers can refine

Small details. A meaningful difference.

The original treatment description focuses on refining the visible characteristics of your smile while keeping the approach conservative.

01

Color

Refine the appearance of tooth color as part of a custom smile design.

02

Shape

Custom-crafted veneers can adjust the visible shape of your teeth.

03

Length

Veneers can be designed to refine tooth length for a balanced appearance.

04

Symmetry

A personalized design can help create a more seamless overall smile.

The process

Designed first. Crafted carefully.

The process typically takes a few visits, moving from digital planning to temporary veneers and finally to the completed porcelain veneers.

Step 01
01

Plan & prepare

First, your smile is mapped out with digital scans and a custom design. Your teeth are then prepared conservatively and impressions are taken.

  • Digital scans
  • Custom smile design
  • Conservative preparation
  • Temporary veneers
Step 02
02

Bond & finish

A couple of weeks later, your final veneers are bonded in place, completing the treatment with a strong and carefully crafted result.

  • Final porcelain veneers
  • Careful fitting
  • Bonding appointment
  • Final smile refinement
A conservative philosophy

Enhance your natural teeth. Don’t replace what makes your smile yours.
