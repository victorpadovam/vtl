His story

Care that starts with understanding.

Before pursuing dentistry, Dr. Adam Gutwein worked as a teacher, a role that shaped his ability to explain dental care in clear, simple terms.

Since founding ATX Family Dental in 2016, he has built a trusted practice in South Austin, known for its patient-first approach and innovative care.

His goal is to combine dental expertise with communication that helps patients make informed, practical decisions about their oral health.
