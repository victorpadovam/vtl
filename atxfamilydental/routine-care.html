Dental cleanings & exams

Preventive care, without the guesswork.

The goal is simple: help you understand what is happening, why it matters, and what you can do between visits.

We’ve put a lot of thought into your hygiene appointment to ensure the best experience possible. Our team upholds high standards of care, and our Registered Dental Hygienists are committed to educating you every step of the way. We believe that being clear, kind, and thorough is the best approach to patient care.

During your cleaning, we may take digital X-rays and intraoral photos if needed, so we can provide a full picture of your dental health. Our goal is for you to leave feeling confident and well-informed, with a clear understanding of what’s happening and why it matters.

In our South Austin office, routine cleanings are designed to eliminate plaque and calculus buildup around your teeth and below the gumline. By removing harmful bacteria and hard debris, the team helps you work toward optimal oral health and a confident smile.

The best way to avoid serious dental issues is by sticking to a regimen of prevention. Maintaining optimal oral health is important for preventing issues including tooth decay, gum disease, bad breath, and tooth loss.

Your visit will typically begin with low-radiation digital X-rays to detect abnormalities or decay between the teeth and a comprehensive examination of your teeth and gums. If there is anything to address, the team will create a treatment plan customized to your unique oral health needs.

The team then uses both hand instruments and an ultrasonic scaler to gently break up plaque and tartar buildup. You’ll also receive guidance on home care between visits so future cleanings can be easier.
