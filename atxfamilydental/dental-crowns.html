Dental Crowns

A fresh start for a damaged tooth.

A dental crown is like a protective helmet that fits over a damaged tooth, restoring its shape, size and strength.

Crowns are often used when a tooth is cracked, worn down, severely decayed, or after a root canal. They can also improve the appearance of misshapen or severely discolored teeth.

At ATX Family Dental, the goal is more than simply placing a crown. We want your restored tooth to feel comfortable, function naturally and look like it belongs with your smile.

When a crown may help

Protecting a tooth when a filling isn't enough.

A dental crown may be recommended for several different situations depending on the condition and structure of the tooth.

01

Tooth Decay

When decay is too extensive for a regular filling, a crown can provide additional protection and support.

02

Cracked Tooth

A crown can help hold together and protect a tooth affected by a significant crack or fracture.

03

Large Filling

A crown can reinforce a tooth when a large filling has compromised its overall structure.

04

Root Canal

After root canal treatment, a crown is often used to add strength and protection to the treated tooth.

05

Misshapen Teeth

Crowns may improve the appearance of severely misshapen or discolored teeth.

06

Tooth Wear

Teeth significantly worn from grinding or erosion may benefit from restoration of their shape and function.

07

Dental Bridges

Crowns can serve as supportive anchors for dental bridges used to replace missing teeth.

08

Tooth Protection

When a tooth needs additional structural support, a crown can provide a protective restoration.

Your appointment

What to expect.

The crown process at our South Austin office is designed around two appointments, giving your dentist time to prepare the tooth and then carefully fit your custom restoration.

1
Approx. 2 hours

First Appointment

We begin by making sure you're comfortable. The area is numbed, and nitrous is available if you're feeling nervous.

Your dentist will conservatively prepare the tooth to make room for the crown. We then take a digital impression or 3D scan to capture the exact shape of your tooth.

The information is sent to the lab so your custom crown can be created to fit and look natural.

  • Comfort-focused preparation
  • Digital impression or 3D scan
  • Temporary crown placed
2
Approx. 30 minutes

Second Appointment

Once your custom crown is ready, we'll remove the temporary crown and carefully try on the final restoration.

We'll check the fit, appearance and feel to make sure everything is right before completing the treatment.

An X-ray may also be taken to double-check that everything is properly aligned.

  • Temporary crown removed
  • Final crown fitted
  • Fit and appearance checked
