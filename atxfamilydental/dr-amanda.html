Dr. Amanda Vinh brings both skill and heart to dentistry, blending art and science to deliver exceptional care. A proud Austin native, she’s passionate about giving back to the community that shaped her.

After earning her degree from Texas A&M University College of Dentistry, Dr. Amanda found her calling in patient-focused care — helping people feel informed, comfortable, and confident in their dental choices.

She takes a partnership approach, working with you to find the best treatment for your needs. Knowing that dental anxiety is real, she’s also a certified sedation dentist, creating a calm, supportive space for patients who need a little extra ease.
