Why save the tooth?

A root canal can help address a badly infected or decayed tooth while preserving the natural tooth rather than immediately removing it.

After your root canal

Protect the tooth once the treatment is complete.

After a root canal, the treated tooth needs a crown to keep it strong and protected. Because damaged tissue has been removed, the tooth can become more fragile and prone to breaking.

Restoration

A crown restores structure and protection.

A crown helps restore the tooth's structure so it can handle normal chewing and pressure, while giving the tooth a natural, healthy look.
