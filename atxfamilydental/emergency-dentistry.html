Emergency dentistry

Start with relief, clarity, and a plan.

Dental pain is tough, and we know how urgent it feels. Whether you’re a current patient or new to our practice, we make sure to leave room in our schedule each day to see Austinites with tooth or gum pain.

When you come in, we’ll start with a quick but thorough evaluation of the problem area. This includes taking an X-ray and conducting a focused, 15–30 minute examination—what we call a limited exam.

Know the signs

When should you reach out?

A dental emergency is an urgent issue that may require immediate attention to relieve pain, prevent further damage, or protect oral health.

  • Knocked-out tooth — seek care as soon as possible.
  • Broken or cracked tooth — especially with severe pain or sensitivity.
  • Sharp, intense dental pain — may signal an underlying dental problem.
  • Suddenly painful, swollen gums — may indicate infection or an abscess.
