Privacy Policy

A clear explanation of how information submitted through the Patrick's Pet Cleanup website may be used.

Last updated: September 24, 2026

This policy is written for the website experience and quote-request flow. If the business adopts additional tools, payment processors, analytics services or customer-management systems, this policy should be updated to reflect those services.

1. Information we collect

When you request a quote or contact Patrick's Pet Cleanup, you may submit information such as your first and last name, email address, phone number, service address, number of dogs, preferred service frequency, sanitization preference, timing of the last cleanup and any message you choose to send.

2. How we use information

Information submitted through the website may be used to respond to quote requests, provide service and pricing information, communicate about appointments or inquiries, and coordinate requested pet waste removal services.

3. Information sharing

Patrick's Pet Cleanup does not sell submitted contact information. Information may be shared with service providers when reasonably necessary to operate the website, process a request, communicate with you or provide the requested service.

4. Data & security

Reasonable measures should be used to protect information submitted through the website. However, no website or electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

5. Cookies & analytics

The website may use cookies or similar technologies provided by the website platform or third-party services. These technologies can help with site functionality, performance and understanding how visitors use the website. Any analytics or advertising tools added in the future should be disclosed here.

6. Your choices

You can choose not to submit optional information. You may also contact Patrick's Pet Cleanup to ask about information previously submitted through the website or to request that communications stop, subject to information that must be retained for legitimate business or legal purposes.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, contact Patrick's Pet Cleanup at info@patrickspetcleanup.com or (512) 589-9584.
