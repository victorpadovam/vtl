B. Information Collected Automatically

We use the collected information to:

By providing your phone number, you agree to receive SMS messages from us, including appointment reminders, service updates, and special offers. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies.

  • Opt-Out: You may unsubscribe at any time by replying STOP to any message.
  • Help: Reply HELP for assistance.

4. Refund, Cancellation & Service Pause Policy

A. Cancellations

We do not require contracts, and customers may cancel services at any time. However, for customers on a quarterly prepayment plan, all prepaid service months must be completed before cancellation.

If a customer chooses to cancel or pause service beyond 90 days, we will resume and complete all remaining prepaid services before cancellation is processed. If service cannot be completed due to lack of access or the customer declines service, any remaining prepaid service days will be considered forfeited, and no refunds will be issued.

B. Refunds

We offer a refund window between the first scoop and the second service. If you are unsatisfied with our work after the first service, you may request a full refund before the second service takes place. No refunds will be issued after this period.

C. Service Pauses

Customers may request to pause service for up to 90 days. If the pause exceeds this period, all prepaid service months must be completed before cancellation is allowed. If service cannot be completed due to lack of access or the customer declines service, any remaining prepaid service days will be considered forfeited, and no refunds will be issued.

To request a refund, cancellation, or service pause, please contact us at scooptroopersatx@gmail.com or 512-721-8311.
